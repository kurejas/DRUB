# DRUB

A one-shape music instrument for the browser. One shape morphs through six screens — rhythm, pad, sequencer, bass, filter, tape stop — while a drum loop, melody and bass play in five styles (2-Step, Dubstep, Drum & Bass, Techno, Deep House), on a synth or recorded acoustic kit.

**Play:** https://kurejas.github.io/DRUB/ — tap the triangle to start. Works best in Chrome or Safari, on a phone or desktop; add it to the home screen to run full screen.

Sounds: synthesised in the page, plus recordings — The Open Source Drumkit (public domain), VCSL (CC0), University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples, Karoryfer Meatbass (CC0). Fonts: SF Pro on Apple devices (system), Archivo elsewhere (Google Fonts).
